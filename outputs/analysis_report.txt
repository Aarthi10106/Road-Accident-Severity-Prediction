LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
The dataset covers reported personal-injury collisions recorded by police; it does not represent every road incident.
Missing values are reported, not automatically imputed.
Several categorical fields may be numeric codes. Consult the official data guide before assigning descriptions to those codes.
Collision counts alone do not measure risk because traffic volume and distance travelled are not included in this analysis.
Observed associations do not prove that a particular condition caused a collision.
